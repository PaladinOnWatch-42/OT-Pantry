## Choose the Control Class. Keep the Architecture.

Start with the control performance your application demands today. Scale capability, connectivity, and intelligence seamlessly within the same PACSystems architecture as needs evolve.

![img](https://www.emerson.com/is/image/emerson/pacsys-img-200-series-plcs:1-1?fmt=webp-alpha) ![img](https://www.emerson.com/is/image/emerson/pacsys-img-cpe400%20series%20plcs:1-1?fmt=webp-alpha) ![PACSystems™ RSTi-EP I/O](https://www.emerson.com/is/image/emerson/rsti-ep-io-angle:16-9?fmt=webp-alpha)

## Slice-Based I/O

Enable high‑density I/O at the machine or process edge with slice-based I/O (also known as RSTi-EP I/O) that simplifies expansion, reduces wiring complexity, and integrates across PACSystems architectures.

![PACSystems™ RX3i I/O](https://www.emerson.com/is/image/emerson/rx3i-io:16-9?fmt=webp-alpha)

## Rack-Based I/O

Implement scalable, high-capacity I/O (also known as RX3i I/O) with rack‑based systems that centralize control, support extensive expansion, and integrate across complex PACSystems architectures.

![icon](https://www.emerson.com/is/image/emerson/pacsys-icon-open-by-design:1-1?fmt=webp-alpha)

##### Open by Design

Integrate across systems, devices, and vendors without restriction while getting support for leading industrial protocols for flexible architectures that easily evolve with changing needs.

![icon](https://www.emerson.com/is/image/emerson/pacsys-icon-defense-in-depth:1-1?fmt=webp-alpha)

##### Defense-in-Depth

Mitigate security gaps at the control layer by protecting your control systems from day one with hardware-backed security (TPM, secure boot) and a defense-in-depth architecture.

![icon](https://www.emerson.com/is/image/emerson/pacsys-icon-one-engineering-environment:1-1?fmt=webp-alpha)

##### One Engineering Environment

Standardize development across all controller classes with a common runtime, reuse code to reduce engineering effort, and simplify commissioning and lifecycle management.

![icon](https://www.emerson.com/is/image/emerson/pacsys-icon-scalable-architecture:1-1?fmt=webp-alpha)

##### Scalable Architecture

Start with compact control and seamlessly expand to higher performance, data-intensive applications using the same backplane--no rearchitecting the system.

![icon](https://www.emerson.com/is/image/emerson/pacsys-icon-data-Driven-operations:1-1?fmt=webp-alpha)

##### Data-Driven Operations

Turn data into insights with the ability to connect to analytics platforms and enable local processing at the edge to support new business models for OEMs.

![icon](https://www.emerson.com/is/image/emerson/pacsys-icon-no-re-platforming-required:1-1?fmt=webp-alpha)

##### No Re-Platforming Required

Protect your engineering investment by modernizing as requirements change without rewriting code, retraining teams, or replacing platforms.

![pacsys-img-one-toolchain-total-control.png](https://www.emerson.com/is/image/emerson/pacsys-img-one-toolchain-total-control:4-3?fmt=webp-alpha)

## One Toolchain. Total Control.

Configure and optimize PLCs and I/O in one unified environment to streamline engineering, reduce complexity, and accelerate deployment across every PACSystems architecture.
