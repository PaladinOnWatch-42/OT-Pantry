## Scalable Secure Control

ThinManager® is widely known for the centralized management and control of applications and visualization resources. Also regarded as the world leader in managing diskless thin client terminals and mobile devices on the plant floor. But ThinManager is so much more than just thin client management. Often thought of as the operating system of the plant floor, ThinManager manages devices, users, and the visual content they rely on to be productive. ThinManager manages the configuration and control of your facility in a truly scalable and reliable modern solution.

## Build the Ultimate Control Room

Powerful visualization and flexible configuration options make ThinManager the simplest and best way to build a control room that can actually see the entire operation in real time. Terminal Shadowing, direct live camera feeds, tiling, custom Virtual Screen dashboards are all ways to build your visualization. Our freeform MultiMonitor wizard makes the setup of any control space as easy and quick as possible. Do you want to have different layouts for different Active Directory groups or users? We can handle that too.

## Scalable Hardware for Thin Client Management

ThinManager is known for the wide range of hardware that it can interact with, shadow, boot, and manage. There are 2 types of classifications for hardware with ThinManager; “ThinManager Ready” and “ThinManager Compatible”.

ThinManager Ready thin clients and industrial PCs come, out of the box, looking for your ThinManager configuration. Up and running in just minutes! This is true for terminal replacement as well as expansion.

Our ASEM® industrial computers, monitors, and thin clients integrate with ThinManager software and are ideal for ThinManager distributed applications. You can have multiple thin clients or computers set up across your plant or facility with various roles. All these devices can be conveniently maintained by the ThinManager server.
