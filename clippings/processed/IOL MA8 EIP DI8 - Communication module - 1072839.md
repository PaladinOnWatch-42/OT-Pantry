## IOL MA8 EIP DI8 - Communication module 1072839

![Product photo Communication module IOL MA8 EIP DI8](https://caas.phoenixcontact.com/caas/v1/stable/media/213192/full/b408?format=jpg)

Eight-channel IO-Link master provides convenient configuration of IO-Link devices using web-based management. Supports connectivity to EtherNet/IP, MODBUS, and OPC UA. Features eight auxiliary digital inputs, redundant input power supply connections, plug-in push-in terminals.

Discontinued, no stock replenishment after Apr 30, 2027

## Product details

---

Provides connectivity to EtherNet/IP, MODBUS TCP, and OPC UA networks. It enables the operation of up to eight IO-Link sensors/actuators and is also used to acquire digital signals. The device is designed for use in systems manufacturing.
