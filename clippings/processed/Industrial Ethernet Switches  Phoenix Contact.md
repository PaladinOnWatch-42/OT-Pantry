## Unmanaged switches

---

Industrial unmanaged switches from Phoenix Contact feature variable numbers of ports and a narrow design. We provide unmanaged switches with a variety of functions, depending on the application.

- **For standard applications:** The 1000 series unmanaged switches feature compact designs and flexible installation options. The 1100 versions also have transmission speeds in the Gigabit range. Using the mounting accessories, you can also mount the FL SWITCH 1000 flat in the control cabinet or on the wall.
- **For field installation:** The 1600 and 1700 series with IP67 degree of protection are particularly resistant to environmental influences.
- **In 19" format:** The 1800 and 1900 series unmanaged switches are ideal for use in server rooms and control rooms.
- **With Power over Ethernet (PoE):** The 1000 series unmanaged PoE switches enable you to supply up to eight end devices with power and data via the same Ethernet cable.
- **For building automation:** DIN rail-mounted switches enable space-saving installation directly in the building distribution board and are available in Gigabit and PoE versions.

## Managed switches for universal automation applications

---

With their easy startup and the large number of versions, the managed switches of the 2000 and 5900 range of product ranges enable the cost-effective management of your Ethernet network. In addition to extended functions, they also enable communication via fiberglass and are equipped with numerous security features.

- **For DIN rail mounting:** Versions 2000, 2100, 2200, and 2300 can be mounted as is usual on the DIN rail.
- **In the 19" format:** The 5900 range switches are suitable for rack mounting and form the ideal interface between OT and IT.
- **With routing functionality:** The NAT versions of the 2000 range ensure the easy connection of machines and systems with the same IP address range to the higher-level network. They combine switching and routing in one DIN rail device.
- **For field installation:** With IP67 degree of protection, the 2600 and 2700 versions are suitable for use in the field and are particularly compact and robust.
