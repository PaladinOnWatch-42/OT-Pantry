## Cloud-based remote maintenance Simple – secure – reliable

The mGuard Secure Cloud provides you with a turnkey and efficient comprehensive solution for remote maintenance. You can connect quickly and securely to machines, industrial PCs, and controllers via a simple web interface.

By using VPN connections, service employees can access the devices anywhere in the world and carry out maintenance work without having to be on site.

**Advantage**: This saves time and costs and increases the efficiency of maintenance processes.

## Overview of the remote maintenance structure Interaction of all components

Service personnel can use a free OpenVPN software client on the service computer or tablet and connect the workstation to the cloud.

The machines are equipped with industrial-grade remote maintenance routers or PLCnext Control, and establish your VPN connection to the mGuard Secure Cloud as necessary. VPN connections can be established locally via hardware (e.g., key switch) or via software controlled by the system operator.

Remote machines and systems can be connected using all modern technologies such as 5G, 4G, fiberglass, etc.

## China VPN+ Encrypted VPN connections to China

The mGuard Secure Cloud provides an officially approved solution for VPN connections to China with 99% availability, available as a flat rate service.

The China VPN+ solution enables highly secure, encrypted communication with mGuard VPN connections via the provider’s backbone. The data is transmitted end-to-to-end encrypted via the Chinese National Network Security without affecting security and data integrity.

**Advantage**: We provide a trusted and regulatory-approved remote access solution to your machines and systems in China.

## Available options From free to scalable

The basic version of the mGuard Secure Cloud with two service connections and a data volume of 8 GB is available free of charge.

You can add additional options to suit your individual maintenance concept on an annual basis, such as:

- **Remote Access+**: 1 additional service connection with a monthly transmission volume of 4 GB
- **China VPN+**: secure and officially approved connections
- **Traffic+ 1000**: 1 TB of additional traffic per client
- **Traffic+ 100**: 100 GB of additional traffic per client
- **Traffic+ 10**: 10 GB of additional traffic per client
- **Support+**: Contact via email and telephone during regular business hours

**Advantage**: you have full flexibility and save on the work for maintaining and updating your systems. This allows you to invest your resources in your core business.
