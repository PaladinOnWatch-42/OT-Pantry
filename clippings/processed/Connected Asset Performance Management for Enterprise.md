## The “Essentials” for Your Assets

![The “Essentials” for  Your Assets](https://www.gevernova.com/software/sites/default/files/inline-images/essentials.webp)

GE Vernova’s Power and Energy Resources business is building modernized cloud-based asset performance management software for asset-intensive organizations across the globe. As the market continues to demand better ingestion, management, and outcomes from more data than ever before—GE Vernova’s SaaS Asset Performance Management (APM) is built on a microservice-based infrastructure that helps organizations achieve more with its data.  
  
Our Essentials platform uses the latest and greatest technology to enhance an organization’s ability to connect to data sources, create and manage workflows, bring and deploy analytics, expand [Asset Performance Management](https://www.gevernova.com/software/products/asset-performance-management) to robotic monitoring, and ultimately connect workers across the globe to insights that reduce failures and operational risk. With Essentials you can:

- Reduce unplanned downtime and maintenance costs
- Deploy faster, scale enterprise-wide
- Enable continuous updates, zero on-prem maintenance
- Align OT data with IT strategy for smarter operations
- Create an essential foundation for AI/ML workflows
