With CODESYS, you can freely select the optimum hardware for your automation application. Many classic automation providers have already implemented CODESYS. Or you can use standard device platforms on which you simply install or deploy the appropriate control software. With virtual controllers, you can even use powerful IT hardware for control and thus reduce hardware costs.

CODESYS offers integrated solutions for many industrial automation tasks. This allows you to create graphical user interfaces, exchange data with field devices or other systems, or configure movements of coordinated axes in parallel with logic programming.

The aim of the system: Whatever you want to automate - with CODESYS you can implement it simply and practically in software. The software is designed in such a way that you can succeed just as quickly with minimal knowledge of automation technology as experienced software professionals who use the full range of features.

Open interfaces, comprehensive security features, convenient connection to a cloud-based administration platform: All this makes CODESYS the natural platform for Industry 4.0/IIoT. Are your controllers located at the field level of machines or systems? Do you use edge controllers or even virtual controllers on private or public servers? CODESYS offers the right control software for every device platform. With the CODESYS Development System, you can configure all controllers equally and exchange data easily in any IIoT network. At the same time, you can embed CODESYS seamlessly into your IT infrastructure to make optimal use of the available resources.
