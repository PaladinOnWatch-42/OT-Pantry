### Version: 3.5.20.0

Service packs for CODESYS V3 are released at annual intervals and include numerous improvements and bug fixes. This version adds support for pragmas in the declaration section. Logging into a device with an application that brings a lot of files has been optimized. It will also include updated offline help.

Known Restrictions

TI DSP: The platform is not available for 3.5.20.0

PPC VLE: The VLE variant of the PPC compiler could not be tested for this release. Therefore, we discourage the use of the 3.5.20.0 PPC compiler on VLE platforms.

March 19, 2024
