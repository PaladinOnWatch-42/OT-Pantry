## Controls hardware and software for every application

The Mark VIe integrates controls across the entire plant into one common controls philosophy, helping operators make faster decisions and respond more quickly to changing conditions.

[Mark VIe turbine controls](https://www.gevernova.com/gas-power/products/digital-and-controls/mark-vie-ecosystem/mark-vie-unit-control)

Tailored with custom configurations and algorithms, and engineered for all GE Vernova turbine types, including aeroderivative, heavy duty, steam, hydro, and wind.

[Mark VIe distributed control system (DCS)](https://www.gevernova.com/gas-power/products/digital-and-controls/mark-vie-ecosystem/mark-vie-distributed-control-system)

A flexible, scalable distributed control system for full-plant operations on a single platform. Supports third-party integration and plant-wide operability.

[ControlST\* software suite](https://www.gevernova.com/gas-power/products/digital-and-controls/mark-vie-ecosystem/st-software-suite)

An integrated software suite that connects every controller, I/O pack, and HMI in a Mark VIe system, providing plant teams with one tool for programming, configuration, trending, and diagnostics.
