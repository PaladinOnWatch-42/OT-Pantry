## Protect the environment and your reputation

Oil and gas pipelines are critical assets. Ensure the safety of the population and the environment where pipelines run.

AVEVA Pipeline Integrity Monitor offers you a holistic view of midstream pipelines to help you prevent, detect, and mitigate the impact of commodity releases.

## Benefits

### Prevent leaks from occurring

Over/under pressure detection analysis helps prevent commodity releases.

### Detect releases quickly

The faster you identify releases like rupture or theft, the faster you can stop them.

### Mitigate the impact

Locate leaks and estimate lost volume to tailor your response.

### Meet regulatory requirements

Conform to API standards and PHMSA regulations.

## Features

AVEVA Pipeline Integrity Monitor is an integrated solution for computational pipeline monitoring (CPM) to detect and mitigate commodity releases.

### Multiple CPM methodologies

Real-time transient model (RTTM), compensated volume balance (CVB), negative pressure wave (NPW), and rate of change combination (ROCC) monitoring—all in a single solution.

### Ready for tomorrow’s commodities

Pipeline Integrity Monitor is ideal for a broad range of commodities needed for the energy transition, including hydrogen, CO<sub>2</sub>, ammonia, and blended fluids.

### Layered leak detection and smart alarms

Flexible integration with hardware-based leak detection sensors provides an intelligent system with a single interface.

### Expert service and experience

AVEVA has been designing pipeline leak detection systems for over 25 years.
