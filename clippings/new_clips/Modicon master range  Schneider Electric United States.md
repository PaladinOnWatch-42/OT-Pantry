## Modicon

Modicon IIoT-native edge controllers manage complex interfaces across assets and devices or directly into the cloud, with embedded functional safety and cybersecurity. Modicon provides performance and scalability for a wide range of industrial applications, up to high-performance multi-axis machines and high available redundant processes.
